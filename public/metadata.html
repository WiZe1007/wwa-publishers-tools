    What gets cleaned

    • Images: Original EXIF, GPS, XMP, ICC and other tags are discarded when the image is re-saved. Dimensions are preserved. JPEG/WebP compression may slightly affect quality; removing color profiles may affect colors.
    • MP4 / MOV / M4V: Standard container metadata blocks are removed and creation/modification dates are reset. Video and audio are not re-encoded.
    • Originals stay unchanged. Cleaned copies use the _clean suffix. ZIP timestamps are standardized.

    Animated PNG/WebP and other formats are not supported. Cleaning does not hide filenames, visible text, faces or data within audio/video streams, and does not guarantee anonymity.
